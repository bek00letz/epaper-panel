Giving the editor its flow back

The editor can do 77 things, and nearly all of them are worth keeping. What broke is where they live: the four things you work on (the panel, the rotation, a page, an element) are each spread across three or four places, saving is invisible, and the canvas doesn't do what your hand expects.

Below: what I found, five everyday tasks counted today and in each direction, and the decisions I need from you. The three directions are clickable in the tabs above; each has a phone view. My recommendation is B.

Your three frustrations

A. Overlapping items. Every click goes to the topmost box. Fix: pick from what's under the pointer, click again without moving to go one deeper, with the whole stack outlined and a “2 of 3” label so you can see you're picking something else. Plus a visible layer list and a “Select” menu on right-click. Try it under Fixes.
B. A click nudges. A drag starts only after 4 px of movement (8 on touch). A plain click never moves anything or adds an undo step.
C. Copying between pages. Copy and paste keeps position and size, works across pages, reloads and windows. “Copy to page…” and “Match layout of…” for the whole job. Saving gets a version check: changes from another window merge by page, and only a page changed in both asks you which to keep.

Also proposed

  • Now on the panel: up to date or waiting (with the reason), how many pages are rotating and how often, which conditional pages are hidden right now and why (“Dryer is idle”), quiet hours, Sleep now, Clear ghosting. It won't claim which page is on screen, because the add-on can't know that.
  • Rotation as a list you drag. A page can sit in it twice, as today; pages left out of it are kept to one side.
  • First-run states: no pages yet (presets right on the canvas), no panel yet (Now on the panel says so and opens setup).
  • The panel's device settings (Sleep, ghost clearing, refresh interval) in Settings. The add-on would find these entities the same way it finds the refresh interval now, by registry id.
  • One sheet system for presets, settings, icons and naming. The seven browser prompts go; rename happens in place.
  • Saving you can see: a dot on “Send to panel” when something isn't sent, a warning before closing with unsent work, and a draft kept in the browser.

What doesn't earn its place

  • Model picker and refresh slider above the canvas: they duplicate Panel setup. Move to Settings.
  • The “1-bit monochrome” and resolution badges, and the fake “ELECROW…” logo on the frame. Keep the dark frame itself.
  • The tip paragraph under the canvas: replaced by a keyboard shortcuts sheet (?) and controls that explain themselves.
  • The eight entity filter chips: search covers it, with “The add-on's own” as one scope.
  • Font scale presets (five buttons next to a slider): a slider that clicks at those values.
  • “Save page as preset”: it has been silently losing your presets (see below). Either fix it, or drop it in favour of Duplicate page and copy and paste.

Bugs found while reading

  • Saved presets disappear on the next save: the editor writes title, the server keeps only presets with name.
  • Every preset arrives unbound, though each was written to take your sensor.
  • Opening Full strip saves all your unsent changes, as do five other actions.
  • The entity list never refreshes after a save (it reads a field that doesn't exist).
  • The editor's own fonts 404 under Home Assistant, so the font menu can't show them.
  • The light/dark menu item loses its label and becomes a bare emoji.

The full inventory, 77 items marked constant, occasional or rare, is in docs/editor-redesign/inventory.md on the editor-redesign branch. It's the regression checklist.

Decisions for you

  1. Direction: A, B or C. I recommend B: the canvas keeps the width inside Home Assistant, the strip under it is the rotation, and on a phone it becomes nearly C.
  2. Picking overlapped items: topmost first and click again for deeper (your idea), or smallest first and click again for bigger (my pick: it fixes “a card blocks what's inside” in one click). Both outline the stack the same way.
  3. Copying between pages: copy, paste and “Copy to page…” only, or also “Match layout of…”, or a live shared layout (bigger: needs the renderer to follow a link).
  4. Saving: keep one “Send to panel” with an unsent dot and a browser draft (my pick, because anything saved reaches the panel at its next fetch), or save automatically as you go.
  5. Two smaller ones: AGENTS.md says controls are at least 36 px; your rule says 44 pt targets. I'd use 44 on touch and 36 with a mouse, with hit areas padded to 44. And with Insert taking over the palette and entity list, the right rail becomes inspector only; AGENTS.md's “ordered by frequency” decision would be rewritten to match.

C · Two windows, one set of pages

Two editor windows on the same pages, like your two tabs. Change something in each and send from both. Different pages merge without asking. If both changed the same page, the second window asks which to keep, and the other version stays one undo away. Copy and paste also works between these windows: ⌘C in one, ⌘V in the other.
